Smart College Complaint Management System

An automated, transparent platform for college students to lodge campus grievances and track resolution progress in real-time. Verified and updated by administrative staff.

How It Works

1

Student Registration

Students sign up with their Roll Number and College Department to access the grievance portal.

2

Lodge Grievance

Submit issues regarding hostels, Wi-Fi, classrooms, sanitation, labs, with priority tags.

3

Admin Verification

Campus admins review, assign, and post official status updates directly from backend authorization.

4

Real-Time Resolution

Students receive immediate updates with ticket tracking and resolution remarks.
